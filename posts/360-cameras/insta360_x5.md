## Overview: pay for recoverable shots, not a new badge

The X5's strongest argument is not that it records 8K. The older X4 already does that. It is that difficult-light footage becomes more useful, and a scratched outer lens becomes a replaceable part rather than an intimidating repair decision. Those are ownership improvements that matter when a camera spends its time on a pole outside a moving person's comfortable reach.

That makes the X5 our balanced adventure recommendation, not our newest-camera recommendation. The X6 now brings higher-end imaging and longer reported 8K runtime, while DJI's Osmo 360 II competes on frame rate and ecosystem convenience. You should still choose the X5 if its complete kit costs meaningfully less and your output does not demand the newer models' specific advantages.

This assessment combines [Insta360's product documentation](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-x5) with [Sam Kieldsen's TechRadar testing](https://www.techradar.com/cameras/360-cameras/insta360-x5-review). Verdict Hound has not handled or tested this camera. The measurements and observations below belong to their named sources; the scores and buying recommendations are our editorial analysis. TechRadar dates the launch to April 22, 2025 and reports a US Standard-bundle launch price of $549.99. That historical reference is not a checked current checkout price.

## Design and build: the exposed glass finally becomes less frightening

The official configuration uses dual 1/1.28-inch sensors, a 2.5-inch touchscreen and a body weighing 200g. The tall layout is familiar to X-series owners. A large preview helps with settings, but remember that a preview of one lens is not the whole final shot. Check both sides for obstructions before recording.

The replacement-lens system is the feature we would prioritize over a cosmetic redesign. Insta360 supplies a replacement procedure and kit for damaged outer lenses. This is not a guarantee against scratches, a promise that every crash is economically repairable, or permission to ignore the instructions. It simply lowers the stakes of the most exposed part of the camera.

There are three separate purchases to understand: a cap for transport, guards for protection during use, and replacement optics if damage happens. A cap cannot protect a camera while recording. Guards can collect smudges or change flare. Replacement lenses restore damaged glass; they do not rescue footage recorded through damage. Inspect and clean the optics before an important session.

The base has a conventional tripod thread and magnetic-mount attachment points. TechRadar found the mounting arrangement useful with an invisible stick. Our advice is to keep camera and pole aligned and to select the mount for the activity rather than merely matching a screw thread. A projecting camera adds leverage; a compact-looking clamp does not automatically make a safe installation.

## Image quality: why the X5 earns more than the X4

Insta360 specifies 8K/30fps spherical recording, 5.7K/60fps and 72MP spherical photographs. These are whole-scene capture figures. They do not mean that a narrow crop pointed toward a distant face carries 8K detail. Reframing chooses a fraction of the sphere, and stabilization, projection and compression also influence the result.

Kieldsen reported visibly clearer, less noisy difficult-light video than the X4, particularly with PureVideo. That is more useful evidence than multiplying sensor dimensions and assuming an equal improvement in every finished clip. Bigger sensors and processing are plausible reasons for the gain, but the buying consequence is simpler: evening material that would be a poor fit for the X4 becomes a more realistic option.

We would use that improvement to widen the times of day at which we recommend shooting, not to promise flawless night action. Denoising can smooth fine textures, and stabilization cannot reverse motion blur already recorded in an exposure. A well-lit subject and a moderate crop remain preferable to a dim subject at the edge of the stitch.

The manufacturer's 5.7K/60fps Active HDR option is especially interesting for daylight motion under mixed shade and bright sky. It trades the maximum pixel count for temporal smoothness and wider-looking tonal coverage. If your final edit is a wide social clip, that can be a better choice than selecting 8K automatically. Test a representative scene and inspect the exported result, not only the camera's small display.

The official page has evolved since launch, including InstaFrame 2.0 information. TechRadar's launch-era coverage describes the earlier flat-output mode. We do not treat an old review's export ceiling as a permanent camera limit. Capture resolution, software export resolution and the detail in a crop are different claims; consult current mode documentation before choosing a delivery workflow.

## Stitching, stabilization and the water distinction

FlowState and Horizon Lock are useful because a spherical capture gives the editor considerable freedom to place a level frame. TechRadar's on-foot and bicycle footage supports the view that the X5 works well as a moving-camera tool. It does not establish that every walking bounce disappears, or that every bike mount stays free of vibration.

Lens joins need thought even when aiming does not. Place the important subject toward a lens, not across the join between them. Close foreground objects seen from two slightly different positions can create awkward seams. A hand wrapped too high around the camera is an avoidable obstruction, not a software failure.

Insta360 rates the body to 15m and recommends its Invisible Dive Case for underwater spherical footage because refraction changes the lens behavior. The case is described as supporting 60m. These are manufacturer specifications, not depths tested by Verdict Hound or independently verified in the cited TechRadar review.

For a buyer, the distinction is between surviving water and producing an acceptable stitched image in water. Budget for the exact case if underwater 360 footage is a central reason to buy. Follow its seal, lens and cleaning instructions. Neither a deeper printed rating nor attractive sample footage removes the need to maintain the equipment.

## Editing and audio: the camera is only part of the purchase

The mobile app makes quick reframing accessible, while Insta360 Studio provides a desktop alternative for people who want a larger timeline and more deliberate control. Keyframing is the real learning task: choose where the viewer looks at meaningful moments, then check the movement between them. More perspective changes are not necessarily better storytelling.

Kieldsen tested the app on an iPhone 13 and encountered stutters, hangs and occasional crashes, although he could finish his edits. Those are source-specific launch-era observations, not proof that all current phones fail. They do justify checking phone compatibility and storage before assuming that a new camera will slot into an older handset's routine.

A desktop route can make longer projects less frustrating, but it is not free of computer requirements or disk use. Keep the original spherical files as well as the finished flat export if you may want another angle later. Deleting the originals to make room removes the main creative advantage you paid for.

The X5 adds a physical wind-guard arrangement and updated audio processing. TechRadar reported that wind interference was reduced rather than eliminated. We would trust that narrower result over language implying silence in all weather. If speech is essential, prioritize microphone placement and a supported external-audio setup; a well-stabilized image with unintelligible narration is still a failed clip.

Basic local reframing and optional cloud services should also be kept separate in your budget. The presence of cloud backup on the product page is not evidence that local editing requires a recurring subscription. Choose those services for their convenience only after checking their current terms and whether you actually need them.

## Battery and recording: don't compare unmatched headline runtimes

TechRadar tested against Insta360's launch-era advertised maximum of 185 minutes (5.7K/24fps Endurance Mode) and cautioned that high-resolution recording drains the battery quickly and makes the camera warm. It did not encounter overheating shutdowns in its review period. Insta360's current official spec page now lists a higher lab-rated maximum of 208 minutes in that same 5.7K/24fps Endurance Mode, and 93 minutes at 8K/30fps (up from 88 minutes at launch). These newer figures are manufacturer lab claims, not numbers TechRadar measured in its hands-on test, and we have not verified them ourselves. We would not convert either the tested 185-minute figure or the newer 208-minute manufacturer claim into a guaranteed uninterrupted indoor 8K session.

Recording mode, airflow, screen use and wireless activity matter. An advertised endurance mode is not the same workload as an 8K/30fps capture test. The later [X6 announcement](https://www.insta360.com/blog/insta360-x6-launch.html) explicitly discusses 140 minutes at 8K/30fps; that is the kind of mode labeling required before a runtime comparison becomes meaningful.

Fast charging helps between sessions. TechRadar reports the X5 reaching 80% in about 20 minutes with a suitable fast charger. That does not mean it fills completely in the same time or works identically with every power source. A spare charged battery is the more predictable solution if the next recording cannot wait.

For a trip, our practical recommendation is to plan footage offloads alongside charging. Storage exhaustion can stop recording even when the battery is fine. Keep clips purposeful, review lens cleanliness after travel, and avoid making a single very long high-resolution take the only record of an event.

## Alternatives: X4, X6 and both DJI generations

The [X4](/articles/insta360_x4) is the cheaper-daylight alternative when the actual local price supports that description. It already has 8K/30fps and a substantial screen. Keep an existing X4 if most footage is outdoors in good light and lens replacement is not a deciding concern. The X5's extra capability is real without being compulsory.

The [X6](/articles/insta360_x6) is the premium option for a creator who wants its newer imaging, internal storage and stronger reported runtime at 8K/30fps. Its higher launch price and the reported beta-app instability mean it is not automatically the better total purchase. Resolve your editing-device fit first.

The [original Osmo 360](/articles/dji_osmo_360) offers internal storage and DJI microphone integration, but its non-replaceable lenses are a meaningful ownership disadvantage. The [Osmo 360 II](/articles/dji_osmo_360_ii) addresses that and adds native 8K/60fps. Its availability and thermal caveats deserve attention, especially if you are comparing imported equipment or want long high-frame-rate takes.

None of these comparisons depends on adopting another publication's star rating. Our six metrics balance useful images, stitching, hardware, workflow, recording and value. The X5 earns its strongest hardware score for lens repairability, while mobile editing friction and mode-dependent recording prevent an unqualified recommendation.

## Who should buy it, and who should skip it

Buy the X5 for travel, outdoor storytelling and moving shots that extend into evening, particularly if a sensibly priced kit leaves room for a stick, storage and a spare battery. It is also a rational replacement for an older 5.7K camera when both better cropping latitude and difficult-light capability matter.

Skip it if the camera will mostly point forward and you rarely intend to reframe. Standard action-camera capture may be a better match, but those products are not equivalent 360 alternatives. Also skip the upgrade from X4 if your current clips already meet your needs. Buying another camera to avoid learning the editor is unlikely to solve the actual problem.

## Verdict

The X5 makes two persuasive improvements to the experience of owning a 360 camera: more usable difficult-light footage and a better response to damaged glass. That combination keeps it relevant below the X6 rather than simply turning it into yesterday's flagship.

Choose it for balanced adventure use, with eyes open about phone performance and recording conditions. Its recommendation is strongest when the complete purchase leaves enough budget to protect, power and edit it properly.

## FAQ

### Does the X5 make an 8K flat video from every direction?

No. The 8K figure describes spherical capture. A flat reframe uses only part of that image. Check current export options separately; increasing an export's pixel dimensions does not manufacture missing subject detail.

### Should an X4 owner upgrade?

Only if the newer capabilities address a real limitation. Difficult light and replaceable lenses are stronger reasons than daylight resolution, because both cameras already support 8K/30fps spherical recording.

### Are lens guards the same as replacement lenses?

No. Guards protect the optics during use. Replacement lenses repair damaged outer optics through the specified procedure. Use a clean cap for transport and do not expect any of these to correct footage already recorded through a dirty lens.

### Can it record underwater without a case?

The body has a manufacturer 15m rating, but Insta360 recommends the dive case for clean 360 stitching. Treat refraction and seal maintenance as separate issues from depth.

### Is the maximum battery figure an 8K guarantee?

No. Maximum endurance figures depend on the selected mode and conditions. The independent review warns that continuous high-resolution recording is more demanding; plan spare power instead of assuming a headline maximum.

### Is a newer phone necessary?

Not universally, but compatible software and sufficient processing and storage are necessary. The cited iPhone 13 test had editing problems. Try the current app with a representative clip or use a suitable desktop workflow before committing to phone-only production.

### Can it replace an external microphone?

For casual ambient sound, the built-in setup may suffice. Reported wind reduction is not total wind removal. For important dialogue, inspect the current compatible-microphone list and plan the microphone position.

### Is the X6 always worth paying more for?

No. Its newer imaging, storage and runtime are valuable for specific workloads, but the X5 remains a balanced choice when its full kit is materially cheaper. Compare current total costs, not just launch camera-body prices.
