**scope:** ProductLabR review-article quality recovery via orchestrated A/B experimentation

## Baseline snapshot

- Total reviews: 127
- Under 150 lines: 75

- Placeholder retailer links (`"#"`): 102
- Weakest categories: laptops, monitors, smart-home, wearables, gaming, TVs

## Objective

Massively improve article quality and comprehensiveness while proving a scalable operating model.

## Shared quality rubric (0-100)

1. Depth and completeness — 20
2. Accuracy and evidence quality — 20
3. Originality / anti-generic writing — 15
4. Comparative buying guidance — 10
5. Practical use-case clarity — 10
6. Structure and FAQ completeness — 10
7. Link and data integrity — 10
8. Frontmatter/render compliance — 5

## A/B experiment design

### Arm A — Editorial-first

- Human-led rewrite
- Strict fact-check workflow
- QA agent validation before approval

### Arm B — Pipeline-first

- Agentic draft chain
- Deterministic QA hard gates
- Selective human review for flagged articles and random audits

### Pilot

- 24 articles total
- Stratified across laptops, monitors, and smart-home
- Balanced assignment: 12 in A, 12 in B

## Orchestrator + sub-agent workflow

1. Audit Agent: baseline scoring and prioritization
2. Brief Agent: structured evidence/research packet
3. Draft/Rewrite Agent: comprehensive draft production
4. Editorial Reviewer Agent: quality and clarity pass
5. Fact-check Agent: claim/spec/link verification
6. QA Gate Agent: deterministic pass/fail checks
7. Orchestrator: routing, revision loop control, metric tracking

## Hard QA gates (must pass)

- Minimum depth threshold (word/line target)
- Required section set present
- FAQ minimum count
- No placeholder or non-HTTPS retailer links
- Valid frontmatter and rating breakdown
- Anti-genericity threshold (similarity/phrase checks)

## Success criteria

- Mean rubric score >= 85
- Critical factual error rate <= 0.5/article
- >= 95% hard-gate pass after one revision
- 0 placeholder retailer links
- Throughput decision rule:
  - B wins if quality is non-inferior and throughput >= 2.5x A
  - A wins if it outperforms B quality by >= 10 points

## Rollout rule

- If B wins: scale pipeline-first with human spot audits.
- If A wins: use editorial-first for flagship categories, hybridize long-tail with B.
- Apply in waves across remaining weak categories until corpus is remediated.
